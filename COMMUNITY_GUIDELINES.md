# Community Guidelines

Welcome to DeepScholar! These guidelines help us maintain a respectful, productive community focused on sharing quality research.

## Our Values

- **Accuracy**: We prioritize factual, well-sourced information
- **Respect**: We treat all community members with courtesy
- **Collaboration**: We build on each other's work
- **Integrity**: We give credit and cite sources properly
- **Growth**: We learn from feedback and help others improve

---

## Expected Behavior

### Be Respectful

- Treat others as you'd like to be treated
- Disagree constructively, not combatively
- Welcome diverse perspectives and backgrounds
- Use inclusive language
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries

### Be Helpful

- Provide constructive feedback on briefs
- Answer questions when you can
- Share your knowledge generously
- Guide newcomers patiently
- Acknowledge good work

### Be Professional

- Stay on topic in discussions
- Use clear, appropriate language
- Back up claims with evidence
- Admit when you don't know something
- Correct your mistakes promptly

### Be Honest

- Don't plagiarize or claim others' work
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Report bugs and issues
- Give credit where it's due
- Be transparent about limitations

---

## Content Standards

### What We Welcome

✅ **Research-Focused Content**
- Academic research summaries
- Scientific explanations
- Technical analyses
- Industry reports
- Educational materials

✅ **Well-Cited Work**
- Properly attributed sources
- Links to original research
- Clear references
- Acknowledgment of contributors

✅ **Constructive Discourse**
- Thoughtful reviews
- Helpful suggestions
- Questions seeking clarification
- Corrections with sources
- Alternative perspectives

### What We Don't Allow

❌ **Misinformation**
- Deliberately false information
- Conspiracy theories
- Pseudoscience presented as fact
- Cherry-picked data to mislead
- Unsubstantiated medical claims

❌ **Harassment**
- Personal attacks
- Hate speech
- Discriminatory content
- Threats or intimidation
- Doxxing or privacy violations

❌ **Spam and Abuse**
- Excessive self-promotion
- Repetitive posting
- Malicious links
- Automated spam
- Vote manipulation

❌ **Inappropriate Content**
- Sexually explicit material
- Graphic violence
- Illegal content
- Malware or viruses
- Scams or fraud

❌ **Plagiarism**
- Copying without attribution
- Presenting others' work as your own
- Insufficient citation
- Copyright violation

## Creating Quality Briefs

### Do:

- ✅ Research thoroughly before writing
- ✅ Cite all sources properly
- ✅ Fact-check AI-generated content
- ✅ Write clear, organized content
- ✅ Update briefs when information changes
- ✅ Respond to feedback professionally
- ✅ Use appropriate categories and tags
- ✅ Proofread before publishing

### Don't:

- ❌ Copy-paste without attribution
- ❌ Make unsupported claims
- ❌ Ignore factual errors
- ❌ Publish deliberately misleading content
- ❌ Use click-bait titles
- ❌ Spam tags or categories

---

## Reviewing and Feedback

### Writing Helpful Reviews

**Good Reviews:**
- Focus on content quality
- Provide specific examples
- Suggest improvements
- Acknowledge strengths
- Maintain respectful tone

**Example:**
```
"This brief provides a clear overview of the topic. The section
on methodology was particularly helpful. Consider adding more
recent sources (2023-2024) to strengthen the analysis."
```

**Unhelpful Reviews:**
- Personal attacks on author
- Vague criticism
- Off-topic comments
- One-word responses
- Spam or trolling

### Responding to Feedback

**When you receive feedback:**
- Thank reviewers for their time
- Consider suggestions objectively
- Ask for clarification if needed
- Implement valuable suggestions
- Explain your reasoning politely

**When feedback seems unfair:**
- Stay calm and professional
- Respond to content, not tone
- Provide evidence for your position
- Agree to disagree if needed
- Report truly abusive comments

## Moderation

### Reporting Content

**Report content that:**
- Violates these guidelines
- Contains misinformation
- Harasses users
- Infringes copyright
- Includes illegal material

**How to Report:**
1. Click the "Report" button on the content
2. Select the reason for reporting
3. Provide additional context if helpful
4. Submit the report

### What Happens Next

1. **Review**: Moderators review all reports
2. **Action**: Violations result in content removal and/or account warnings
3. **Appeals**: Authors can appeal decisions
4. **Transparency**: Serious violations are documented

### Enforcement Actions

**First Violation (Minor)**
- Warning and explanation
- Request to edit or remove content
- Education about guidelines

**Repeated or Serious Violations**
- Content removal
- Temporary account suspension
- Permanent account ban (severe cases)

**Immediate Actions (Severe Cases)**
- Spam: Instant removal
- Harassment: Immediate suspension
- Illegal content: Account termination + report to authorities

## Intellectual Property

### Copyright

- Only post content you have rights to share
- Respect others' intellectual property
- Provide proper attribution
- Don't republish paywalled content
- Fair use applies, but be conservative

### Licenses

- Your content: You retain ownership
- Platform license: You grant DeepScholar rights to display your content
- Others' work: Respect their licenses
- AI-generated content: Be aware of model terms

### Citations

**Always cite when you:**
- Quote directly
- Paraphrase ideas
- Present specific data
- Reference studies
- Build on others' work

---

## Privacy and Safety

### Protect Your Privacy

- Don't share personal information publicly
- Use privacy settings appropriately
- Be cautious about identifying yourself
- Protect others' privacy too

### Protect Others

- Don't dox or reveal personal information
- Respect professional boundaries
- Report safety concerns
- Support victims of harassment

### Account Security

- Use strong, unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication (when available)
- Don't share account credentials
- Report suspicious activity

---

## Academic Integrity

### Honesty in Research

- Represent sources accurately
- Don't fabricate data
- Disclose limitations
- Acknowledge uncertainties
- Note when information is preliminary

### Conflicts of Interest

**Disclose when:**
- You're affiliated with organizations mentioned
- You have financial interests in the topic
- You're reviewing competitors' work
- You have personal relationships affecting objectivity

---

## Inclusivity and Accessibility

### Inclusive Communication

- Use gender-neutral language when appropriate
- Avoid ableist language
- Respect cultural differences
- Make content accessible to non-experts when possible

### Accessibility

- Use clear, simple language
- Provide alt text for images (when supported)
- Structure content with proper headings
- Avoid walls of text
- Define technical terms

## Appeals Process

### If You Disagree with a Moderation Decision:

1. **Review**: Read the specific guideline cited
2. **Reflect**: Consider if the decision is fair
3. **Appeal**: Email moderation@deepscholar.com with:
   - Your username
   - Content in question
   - Reason for appeal
   - Evidence supporting your case

4. **Response**: Receive decision within 7 days
